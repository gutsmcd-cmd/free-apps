About the family

Small offline apps by Mat (M@), built in Japan. No account needed.

Free for good. No ads, no subscription, no Pro upsell.

Two families

  • Japanese app family. Tools that feel clumsy in Japanese, rebuilt for everyday Japan. Example: 画像しぼる.
  • General utilities. Clean free remakes of tools that are usually full of ads or logins. Examples: Three Things, Long Shot.

Offline

Each app is a PWA. After one visit you can add it to your home screen. The core works without a network. Files and notes stay on the device. There is no backend waiting for your data.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts
  • No analytics chasing you around the web
  • No ad networks
  • No free-trial-then-paywall bait

Open source

Public apps live at github.com/gutsmcd-cmd. Issues and pull requests are welcome when something is broken or unclear.
