About the family

Small offline apps by Mat (M@). Built in Japan. Shipped as installable web apps you can use without creating an account.

Visa-honest note. I’m not monetizing these apps yet. No ads, no subscriptions, no “Pro” upsell. That is a deliberate constraint — not a temporary teaser for a paywall later.

The pitch

Truly free forever · no login · works offline · no ads. Quality markets itself. If an app is useful, people share it. If it isn’t, no amount of dark-pattern growth will fix that.

Two product lines

  • General utilities — clean free remakes of tools that are usually ad-heavy or login-walled (example: Three Things, Long Shot).
  • Japanese-first alternatives — tools that are great in English but weak or awkward in Japanese UI (example: 画像しぼる).

What “offline” means here

Each app is a Progressive Web App (PWA). After the first visit you can install it to your home screen. Core features are designed to work without a network. Your files and notes stay on your device — we don’t run a backend that needs your data.

What we don’t do

  • No account system
  • No analytics SDKs chasing you across the web
  • No ad networks
  • No “free trial then paywall” bait

Open source

Public apps live under github.com/gutsmcd-cmd. Issues and pull requests are welcome when something is broken or unclear.
